What Could Be Better

Assembly is the recurring frustration. Owners say the instructions could be clearer and the hardware bags aren't always labeled well. That turns a straightforward build into a longer project than expected. The wood-look panels are the other weak point: reviewers describe them as noticeably less rigid than the metal frame, and a few mention slight sagging after heavier items sat on a shelf for weeks. At 33.5 inches tall, the cabinet also takes up floor space that the DUMOS and Kitsure over-the-toilet units don't need, so anyone without room beside the toilet should look at those alternatives instead.

A handful of reviewers also mention that the panel finish scuffs more easily than they expected, particularly around the shelf edges where towels and baskets slide in and out daily. None of this changes the overall stability of the cabinet, but it's worth knowing before you buy if you want the shelves to still look new after a year of regular use.
