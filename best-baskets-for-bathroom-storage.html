Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best basket material for bathroom storage?

Metal wire baskets, like the DEKAVA Under Sink Organizer 2 and the NETEL system, resist moisture and wipe clean fastest, which makes them the safer choice for under-sink cabinets. Woven bamboo and paper-rope baskets, like the StorageWorks and CubesLand picks, look better on an open shelf but should stay away from direct splashes.

How many baskets does a bathroom actually need?

Most bathrooms need two to four baskets split between an under-sink cabinet and a shelf or countertop. Start with one organizer, such as the DEKAVA Under Sink Organizer 2, and add a second basket only if loose items are still piling up after a week.

Are modular basket systems worth the extra cost over a single basket?

A modular system like the NETEL Under Sink Organizers costs more than a single basket, but it lets you split a deep cabinet into separate sections by category instead of mixing everything in one bin. That extra structure pays off in cabinets crowded enough that a single basket keeps getting buried.

Can woven baskets go inside a shower or near a tub?

The woven baskets in this guide, including the StorageWorks bamboo basket and the CubesLand paper-rope basket, are built for dry storage on shelves and counters rather than direct water exposure. If a basket needs to sit somewhere it gets splashed regularly, a metal wire basket like the DEKAVA or NETEL options will hold up better.

Do these baskets fit standard under-sink cabinets?

Cabinet sizes vary enough that you should measure before ordering. The DEKAVA Under Sink Organizer 2 is designed to stack around standard plumbing, while a wider system like the NETEL organizer needs a deeper cabinet to fit all four panels comfortably.
