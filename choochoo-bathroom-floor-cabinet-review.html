What Could Be Better

The ChooChoo listing does not publish a shelf weight capacity, which makes it harder to judge whether it can hold a stack of towels and a basket of toiletries on the same shelf without sagging. Spreading heavier items across shelves rather than stacking them on one is the safer approach until you see how your own load settles.

Assembly is the other weak point reviewers flag most often with metal-and-wood cabinets in this price range. Skipping ahead in the instructions, especially attaching the door before the side panels are square, is the most common way owners report ending up with a door that does not close flush. Plan on 30 to 45 minutes for assembly and keep a level nearby, since an uneven bathroom floor can exaggerate a small alignment issue in the frame.
