The CLEEMAN Bathroom Floor Cabinet is built around a metal frame with wood-panel doors and shelving, a combination that is sturdier than the all-particleboard cabinets common at this price. At 12 inches deep, 31.5 inches wide and 32 inches tall, it sits comfortably against a wall without crowding a small bathroom, and the enclosed doors keep towels and toiletries out of sight instead of stacking them on open shelves.

Owners who left verified reviews consistently mention the cabinet's finish holding up well against bathroom humidity, a detail that matters more than style for a piece that lives next to a shower or tub. The main trade-off in this cleeman bathroom floor cabinet review is price: at $134.99, it sits above two of the three alternatives we compared it against, so buyers on a tighter budget may prefer the Akxomel or Tangkula instead.

The 31.5 inch width is the number that matters most when comparing it against the other three cabinets in this review. It gives the CLEEMAN more usable shelf and door space than the Akxomel's narrower tower or the Tangkula's more compact frame, which is why the extra $15 to $21 over those two alternatives tends to track with more storage rather than just a brand markup. The HMstone sidesteps the comparison entirely by mounting over the toilet instead of sitting on the floor, so its value depends on whether you have floor space to spare in the first place.

What We Liked

In this cleeman bathroom floor cabinet review, the build quality stands out first. A metal frame underneath wood paneling gives the CLEEMAN more rigidity than cabinets built entirely from particleboard, and reviewers consistently note that the doors close flush even after months of use, a detail that is easy to get wrong in flat-pack furniture. The enclosed storage is generous for its footprint too: at 31.5 inches wide, it holds more than the HMstone's narrower over-the-toilet design, and it keeps everything behind closed doors rather than on an open shelf collecting dust. We also like that the finish, per owner reports, resists the humidity and splashing that bathroom furniture takes on daily. The shelving behind those doors is deep enough for stacked towels or cleaning supplies, which is more than you get from the slimmer Tangkula or Akxomel units.

What Could Be Better

Price is the main drawback in this cleeman bathroom floor cabinet review. At $134.99, the CLEEMAN costs more than both the Akxomel and the Tangkula cabinets in this comparison, and buyers shopping strictly by budget will find cheaper options that cover the basics. A second recurring theme in verified owner reviews is the included assembly hardware: several buyers mention swapping the stock screws for sturdier ones to keep the doors from loosening over time. A smaller number of reviewers note that the wood paneling, while attractive, scratches more easily than the metal frame it sits on, so it is worth handling toiletry bottles carefully around the door edges. Neither issue is a dealbreaker, but they are worth factoring into the price before you buy.

Who Is It For?
