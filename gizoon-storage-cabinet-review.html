What We Liked

The Gizoon cabinet solves one problem directly: fitting storage into a bathroom that does not have room for a full-size cabinet. The metal frame is the detail reviewers mention most often, since it holds up better under load than the all-particleboard cabinets common at this price. Several verified reviews describe using it to store towels, cleaning supplies and toilet paper within arm's reach of the toilet, which matches how Gizoon markets the product. The price also holds up well against the competition. At $104.99, it undercuts the $109.99 Homhedy 67 inch H cabinet while offering a similar mix of metal and wood-toned materials, and it lands only a few dollars above the HITNET rattan cabinet, which trades some rigidity for a lighter, more decorative look. Owners also point to the closed cabinet doors as an advantage over open shelving units, since they keep clutter out of sight in a room that guests see regularly.

What Could Be Better

The biggest gap in the Gizoon listing is dimensional detail. Unlike the Homhedy cabinets in this comparison, which advertise a 67 inch height up front, Gizoon's page leaves buyers to dig through photos and the Q&A section to estimate how the cabinet will fit their space. Reviewers who mention assembly describe it as straightforward but note that the included hardware has little tolerance for crooked panels, so squaring each piece before tightening screws matters more than it would with a heavier wood cabinet. A smaller number of owners report that the wood-toned laminate shows scuff marks over time, a tradeoff you would expect at this price versus a solid-wood finish. Buyers comparing this category closely should also weigh that neither Gizoon nor HITNET publishes a shelf weight limit, while the Homhedy listings do, which makes the Homhedy pair easier to plan around if you intend to store heavier items like stacked towels or cleaning supplies.

Who Is It For?

The Gizoon Storage Cabinet fits best in a small bathroom, apartment or rental where floor space is limited and a bulky wood armoire will not fit. It also suits anyone comparing slim storage cabinets on price, since it lands in the middle of the pack among the options in this gizoon storage cabinet review. It is a weaker match for anyone who wants guaranteed exact dimensions before buying, or for buyers who want a heavier, all-wood look rather than a metal-and-laminate build. Those readers are better served by the Homhedy 67 inch H cabinet covered below, or by looking outside this specific comparison altogether. Renters who move often may also prefer it to a built-in solution, since a freestanding cabinet like this one travels with you rather than staying behind with the unit.
