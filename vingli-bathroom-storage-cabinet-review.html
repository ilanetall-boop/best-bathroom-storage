What Could Be Better

Assembly is the recurring frustration. Owners report hardware bags that are not clearly labeled, which turns a straightforward build into a longer project for anyone without flat-pack furniture experience. The wood-look panels are the other weak point: reviewers describe them as noticeably less rigid than the metal frame, and a few mention slight flexing after heavier items sat on a shelf for weeks. As a small floor cabinet, the VINGLI also offers less overall storage than the taller Homhedy alternatives, so anyone prioritizing maximum capacity over footprint should look at those options instead.

A handful of reviewers also mention that the finish scuffs more easily than they expected, particularly around the door edges where it gets opened and closed daily. None of this changes the overall stability of the cabinet, but it is worth knowing before you buy if you want it to still look new after a year of regular use. Owners who line the shelves with a thin mat or liner report fewer scuff marks over time, a simple fix that doesn't show up in the product listing itself.

Who Is It For?

The VINGLI Bathroom Storage Cabinet fits a household with a small bathroom and a specific gap to fill rather than an entire wall to furnish. Its narrow 23.3-inch-wide footprint makes it a strong pick for corners, the space beside a toilet, or a half bath doubling as guest storage. It is not the right choice if you need maximum shelf capacity for a family's worth of towels and toiletries. In that case, the taller Homhedy models covered below make better use of vertical space for the same price. A single person or a couple sharing one bathroom gets the most out of this cabinet, since the enclosed storage covers everyday toiletries without demanding the floor space a larger piece of furniture would need.

Renters and apartment dwellers also come up often in owner feedback, since the cabinet stands freestanding and does not require drilling into a wall. Anyone who has struggled to fit a standard vanity cabinet into a cramped bathroom will notice the difference the VINGLI's narrower profile makes. On the other hand, a household storing bulky items like a full set of bath towels for four people will run out of room faster on this model than on either Homhedy alternative below. If your current bathroom storage consists of a single shelf over the sink, even the VINGLI's modest capacity represents a real upgrade.
