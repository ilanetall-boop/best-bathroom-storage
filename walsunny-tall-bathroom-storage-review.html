What We Liked

The clearest strength shared by the Walsunny tall bathroom storage cabinet and its closest rivals is how much vertical storage they pack into a small footprint. At 64 inches, the Walsunny gives you several shelves of space while occupying a strip of floor barely over a foot wide, and the Gizoon tower pushes that further at 67 inches tall for the same $82.99 price as the Yaheetech. Owners reviewing the Walsunny consistently mention that the metal and wood build feels sturdier than typical all plastic bathroom towers at this price. That narrow price gap is part of why this comparison works: you are not trading up or down in cost, only in footprint and mounting style.

What Could Be Better

None of these cabinets run deep. At 11.8 inches, the Walsunny tall bathroom storage cabinet will not hold large bins or stacks of bath towels without crowding the shelf edge, and the Gizoon and Yaheetech share similarly shallow profiles. Reviewers also flag that assembly takes longer than expected on tall, narrow furniture like this, since the height leaves the cabinet top heavy until every panel is secured. The two separate Walsunny listings add confusion rather than choice, and buyers report ordering the wrong one and receiving a cabinet that looks identical in photos but ships from a different listing page. The extra three inches on the Gizoon tower also means it needs a bit more vertical clearance than the Walsunny, worth checking against your own ceiling height and any shelving above the toilet. If you need deep storage for bulky items, a wider cabinet outside this size class will likely serve you better than any of the four towers in this review.

Who Is It For?

You are a good fit for the Walsunny tall bathroom storage cabinet if your bathroom has a narrow gap beside the toilet, tub or vanity and you would rather add height than lose floor space. Renters and small apartment bathrooms benefit most, since freestanding towers like this and the Gizoon do not require drilling into tile or drywall. If your bathroom has no floor space to spare at all, the Yaheetech over the toilet cabinet is the better match, since it mounts above the tank instead of competing for square footage. If you want the tallest option in this group, the Gizoon's 67 inches beats the Walsunny by three inches for the same $82.99 as the Yaheetech. Skip all four of these options if you need to store large items such as beach towels or bulk toilet paper packs, since their shared shallow depth limits what fits comfortably on each shelf.
